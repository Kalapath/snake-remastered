## Overview

## Information We Collect

### Information you provide
- **Player name:** An optional display name you choose. Used only on the in-game leaderboard.

### Information collected automatically
- **Game scores:** Your high scores are stored on your device and, if the online leaderboard is enabled, submitted to our database (Firebase Firestore) along with your player name.
- **Device information:** Standard analytics data such as OS version and device type may be collected by Firebase automatically.

## How We Use Your Information

- To display your name and score on the global leaderboard
- To save your game progress, owned skins, and coin balance locally on your device
- To provide daily login bonuses

## Data Storage

- **Local data** (scores, coins, settings, skins) is stored exclusively on your device using the browser's localStorage. It is never shared with third parties.
- **Leaderboard data** (name + score) is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, operated by Google LLC. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children's Privacy

This game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game contains no advertising and does not require account registration.

## In-App Purchases
